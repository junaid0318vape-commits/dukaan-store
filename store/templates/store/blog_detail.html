Aapki wrist ke liye sahi watch kaise chunein

Watch khareedte waqt teen cheezein dhyan mein rakhein: sabse pehle apni wrist size ke hisaab se sahi case size chunein — chhoti wrist ke liye 36-40mm aur bari wrist ke liye 42mm+ behtar rehta hai. Doosra, apna lifestyle dekhein: agar aap active hain to fitness watch ya sports watch behtar rahegi, aur formal use ke liye leather strap wali analog watch zyada suit karti hai. Teesra, budget aur warranty zaroor check karein — hamari saari watches par warranty available hai.

Aakhir mein, strap material bhi important hai — leather elegant lagta hai lekin steel ya silicone roz mareez wear aur paani se bachne ke liye zyada practical hote hain. Humari trending watches collection browse karein aur apni pasand ki watch dhoondein.
